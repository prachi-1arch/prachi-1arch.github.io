Case Studies

Customer Churn Prediction

Telecom Industry | 6 Weeks

Business Problem

A telecom client was losing 15% of customers annually to competitors. They needed a predictive system to identify at-risk customers 30 days before churn.

Solution Architecture

Built an end-to-end ML pipeline: SQL Server data extraction → Python ETL → Feature engineering (RFM, tenure segments) → XGBoost classifier → SHAP explainability → FastAPI REST service → Power BI monitoring dashboard.

Results

Achieved 89% precision and 87% recall. Identified 2,400 high-risk customers in first month. Retention campaigns reduced churn by 22%.

AI Document Q&A with RAG

Enterprise Knowledge Base | 4 Weeks

Business Problem

AI Sales Forecasting

Retail Industry | 8 Weeks

Business Problem

A retail chain struggled with inventory overstock and stockouts due to inaccurate demand forecasting across 200+ SKUs and 50 stores.

Solution Architecture

Historical sales ETL → Feature engineering (lag, rolling, seasonal) → LSTM deep learning model + Prophet ensemble → Hyperparameter tuning with Optuna → MLflow experiment tracking → Azure ML deployment → Power BI forecast dashboard.

Results

Improved forecast accuracy from 68% to 87% (MAPE reduced from 32% to 13%). Inventory holding cost reduced by $1.2M annually.
